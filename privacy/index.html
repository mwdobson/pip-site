Pip Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Pip ("we", "us") is an iPhone app for ranking bars, rating drinks, and seeing what your friends think. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Pip is in a small private beta.

Who can see your stuff

Your bar rankings, drink ratings, reviews, and photos are visible only to you and the people you accept as friends. They are never public. Your profile (username, display name) can be found by other Pip users so friends can add you. If you block someone, neither of you can see the other's profile or content.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address (or the private relay address Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple) and a sign-in identifier.
  • Profile: username, display name, and the date you confirmed you are 21 or older.
  • What you add: bar rankings, drink ratings and reviews, photos and captions, friend requests and friendships, blocks, and reports you file.
  • Location: if you allow it, Pip uses your location on your phone to show bars near you on the map. We don't store your location.
  • Diagnostics: if the app crashes, we may receive a crash report (device model, OS version, what the app was doing). It does not include your photos or reviews.

We don't collect contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Photos

Photos are resized on your phone before upload and stored privately. They are shown to you and your friends through links that expire. Photo metadata such as GPS location is not kept.

Services we use

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage; servers in the United States)
  • Mapbox (map tiles; Mapbox receives your device's IP address and the map area you are viewing)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, TestFlight)
  • Expo (app updates) and Sentry (crash reports, if enabled)

These providers process data only to run Pip for us.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Settings → Delete account. This immediately deletes your account, rankings, ratings, reviews, photos, friendships, and blocks. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten. Reports you filed about others are kept without your name so we can keep the community safe.

Age

Pip is only for people who are 21 or older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 21 is using Pip, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, remove rankings, ratings and photos, unfriend or block people, and delete your account in the app. For anything else, including a copy of your data, email us.
