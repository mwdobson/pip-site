Pip Terms of Use

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Pip is an iPhone app for ranking bars, rating drinks and sharing photos with friends. It's in a small private beta. By using Pip you agree to these terms.

Who can use Pip

You must be 21 or older and use Pip only where it's legal for you. One account per person. Keep your sign-in details to yourself.

Your content

You own what you post: rankings, ratings, reviews, photos and drink names. You give us permission to store it and show it to the people the app shows it to. Rankings, ratings, reviews and photos go to you and your accepted friends. Drink names you add are visible to everyone on Pip. Only post photos you took or have the right to share.

No objectionable content or abusive behavior

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Don't post or do anything that is:

  • harassing, bullying, threatening or hateful
  • sexual, violent or graphic
  • illegal, or that encourages illegal or dangerous drinking
  • spam, ads or links
  • someone else's private information

You can report any photo, review, drink name or profile, and block any user. We review reports and may remove content or suspend or delete accounts that break these rules, without notice.

Drink responsibly

Pip is for sharing opinions about bars and drinks. It isn't a reason to drink more. Never drink and drive.

The beta

Pip is a test version. Features can change, break or go away, and data may be reset. Pip is provided "as is", without warranties. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for losses from using the app.

Ending

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Settings → Delete account. We may end access to the beta at any time.
