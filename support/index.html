Common questions

How do I sign in? Use Sign in with Apple, or the email and password you were sent with your beta invite.

I forgot my password. Email us and we'll reset it. (There's no automatic reset during the beta.)

How does ranking work? Pick how a bar was (Loved it, It was fine, or Didn't like it), then answer a few "Which did you like more?" questions. Pip places the bar in your list and gives it a score from 0 to 10.

Who can see my rankings and photos? Only you and people you've accepted as friends.

How do I report or block someone? Open their profile or the photo or review, tap •••, and choose Report or Block. Blocking hides you from each other completely.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything you've added.

Something's broken. In TestFlight, take a screenshot and use Share Beta Feedback, or email us with what you tapped and what happened.
